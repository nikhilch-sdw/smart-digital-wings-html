Client FAQs

Common Questions About Our Google PPC Service

Straightforward answers about budget requirements, tracking, and scaling.

What is the difference between Google Search Ads and Performance Max (PMax)?
Google Search ads capture high-intent users actively typing specific commercial keywords into Google. Performance Max (PMax) uses Google's AI to serve ads across all Google channels (Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Discover, and Maps) from a single campaign using automated audience signals.
How do you stop competitors from clicking our Google Ads?
We deploy automated click fraud software that tracks IP addresses, device fingerprints, and click frequency in real time. If a user or bot clicks your ads repeatedly without converting, their IP address is automatically excluded within milliseconds.
What minimum monthly ad spend do you recommend for Google Ads?
For local service businesses, a minimum ad budget of ₹30,000 to ₹50,000 monthly is ideal. For national e-commerce or B2B SaaS campaigns, we recommend ₹75,000+ monthly to ensure enough conversion data for Google's Smart Bidding algorithms to optimize effectively.
Do we need dedicated landing pages for our Google Ads?
Yes, highly recommended. Sending Google Ads traffic to your homepage reduces conversion rates by 50%+. We build dedicated, high-converting direct-response landing pages where the headline matches the exact keyword the user searched for.
How do you track phone calls and offline sales conversions?
We implement dynamic number insertion (DNI) to track which specific keyword generated each phone call, and sync offline CRM lead status back into Google Ads via the Google Enhanced Conversions API.
Do I retain complete ownership of my Google Ads account?
Yes, 100%. The account belongs to your organization. You grant our agency manager access, and you retain full ownership of all data, campaign structures, and conversion histories indefinitely.
