Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on how we operate, our retainer structure, and realistic scaling timelines.

How quickly can we expect to see tangible ROI results?

For paid ads (Meta & Google), initial conversion lifts typically occur within 14–21 days during creative testing. For Technical SEO and organic authority, noticeable keyword gains emerge between 45–90 days.

Do you require long-term lock-in contracts?

No. We operate strictly on month-to-month retainers. We believe compounding performance should retain clients, not rigid legal lock-ins. 99.4% of our brands choose to stay with us month after month.

What ad budgets do you recommend starting with?

We work with growth-stage brands typically allocating ₹50,000 to ₹10 Lakh+ monthly. Media plans are engineered to achieve cash-flow positive ROAS before scaling spend aggressively.

How often will we receive performance reports and updates?

You receive real-time 24/7 Looker Studio dashboards, weekly Loom video analysis breakdowns, and bi-weekly strategic sprint calls with your dedicated growth director.

What industries and business models do you specialize in?

We specialize in high-growth D2C e-commerce, premium B2B professional services, real estate, healthcare, luxury brands, and solar/clean-tech companies scaling nationwide.

How quickly can you launch our first campaign sprint?

Our standard onboarding takes 72 hours. Once tracking pixels, GA4, and brand assets are verified, initial creative test sets and landing funnels go live within 4 to 6 business days.

What makes Smart Digital Wings different from others?

We combine engineering-grade web speed with luxury creative execution. Senior growth strategists personally oversee your campaigns with 100% transparent attribution and weekly ROI reporting.

Can you handle both web development and ad marketing?

Yes. That is our core advantage. Ad spend is wasted if your website loads slowly or fails to convert. We engineer sub-second landing funnels and simultaneously drive high-intent traffic to them.

Who owns the ad accounts, assets, and creative designs?

You retain 100% full legal ownership of your Meta Business Manager, Google Ads accounts, domains, codebase, and creative assets. If you ever leave, you keep everything.

Can you audit our existing marketing setup before we sign?

Yes. We provide a complimentary 360° growth audit evaluating conversion leaks, tracking pixel health, SEO authority, and competitor benchmarking before any commitments.

How does your agency pricing model work?

We operate on transparent flat-fee monthly retainers or hybrid performance models (base retainer + performance bonus). There are zero hidden onboarding fees or surprise surcharges.

What happens if a campaign underperforms KPIs?

Our rapid-iteration protocol triggers immediate creative swaps, audience recalculations, and landing page split-tests at zero additional management fee until target KPIs are restored.
