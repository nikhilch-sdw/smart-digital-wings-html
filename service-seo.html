Client FAQs

Common Questions About Our Technical SEO

Clear explanations of timelines, link quality, and algorithmic safety.

How long does it take to rank on Page 1 of Google?
For low-to-medium competition terms and existing domains, measurable rank velocity is typically achieved in 60 to 90 days. For high-volume enterprise commercial keywords, competitive top-3 rankings typically mature within 4 to 6 months of steady technical fixes and authority link acquisition.
Are your backlinks 100% white-hat and safe from Google penalties?
Yes, strictly 100% white-hat. We do not use private blog networks (PBNs), automated link builders, or spam forum comments. Every link we acquire is an authentic editorial placement earned via custom digital PR research, expert quotes, and resource outreach to real editors.
How do you adapt to Google Core Updates and AI Overviews?
We engineer deep E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness) signals across your entire site. We implement schema markup, author bios with verified credentials, and structured FAQ entities that directly feed Google's AI Overview summary cards.
Can you recover a website that has experienced a ranking drop or penalty?
Yes. We conduct forensic backlink audits, disavow toxic link spam, resolve manual action notices via Google Search Console, and repair structural algorithmic issues to restore lost organic visibility.
Do you handle on-page content creation or only technical SEO?
We handle both! Our team of expert technical copywriters writes comprehensive, research-backed pillar content, buyer guides, and product comparison pages optimized for both search bots and human conversion.
How will I track ranking progress and organic ROI?
You will receive access to a live Google Looker Studio dashboard updated daily with keyword positions, click-through rates (CTR), impressions, organic conversions, and pipeline revenue tracked through GA4.
