Client FAQs

Common Questions About Our Meta Ads Service

Straight answers to help you make an informed decision for your brand.

What is the minimum ad spend required to work with Smart Digital Wings?
We typically work with brands spending a minimum of ₹35,000 to ₹50,000 monthly on ad spend (excluding agency management fees). This ensures enough statistical volume for our Dynamic Creative Testing (DCT) algorithms to find winning creatives quickly.
Do you produce the ad creatives and video reels, or do we supply them?
Both! We provide direct-response scripting, storyboard briefs, motion graphics, and graphic design in-house. For founder or UGC content, we provide exact word-for-word scripts and direction, then edit your raw footage into studio-grade viral reels.
How quickly can we expect to see profitable ROAS?
Initial creative testing signals are identified within 7 to 10 days. By Day 21, winning DCT combinations are isolated, and sustainable profit scaling begins. Most clients achieve a marked reduction in CPA within their first 30 days.
Do I retain complete ownership of my ad accounts and creative assets?
Yes, 100%. All campaigns are operated inside your own Meta Business Manager. You retain full ownership of all custom audiences, pixels, ad data, and high-resolution video creatives forever.
What happens if our ad account faces a restriction or ban from Meta?
As Certified Meta Media Partners, we have direct enterprise agency support channels. We build backup business manager architectures, enforce strict policy compliance, and can resolve erroneous flags in a fraction of standard wait times.
How do you handle reporting and day-to-day communication?
You will have a private Slack or WhatsApp channel with your dedicated Senior Media Buyer and Account Director. Additionally, you receive a 24/7 live Google Looker Studio dashboard tracking ROAS, CPA, and net revenue updated hourly.
