Client FAQs

Common Questions About Our Brand Identity Service

Everything you need to know about deliverables, ownership, and revisions.

What files and formats do we receive upon project completion?
You receive master vector files (AI, EPS, SVG), high-resolution web formats (PNG, WebP, JPG), PDF print-ready vector packaging dielines, a complete 40-page digital Brand Guidelines manual, and full Figma design system kits.
Do I retain 100% intellectual property ownership of the designs?
Yes, unconditionally. Once final payment is made, all intellectual property rights, trademarks, copyright, and master source files belong entirely to you with zero royalty or usage restrictions.
How many creative concepts and revisions are included?
We present 3 completely distinct creative positioning directions. Once you select your preferred direction, we provide unlimited refinement rounds to ensure every line, font, and color token meets your exact vision.
Do you help with physical packaging manufacturing and print coordination?
Yes. We provide precise technical dielines with exact Pantone color specifications, foil-stamping vectors, and UV spot coatings ready for your packaging supplier. We can also interface directly with your printers to verify pre-press proofs.
Can you rebrand an established company without alienating existing customers?
Yes. We specialize in strategic brand evolution. We retain your core brand heritage and equity while modernizing typography, enhancing contrast, and elevating luxury positioning to appeal to higher-tier enterprise clients.
How long does the entire brand identity process take?
Our standard sprint takes 3 to 4 weeks from initial discovery session to final master asset handover. We work in tight, scheduled milestones to ensure rapid, uncompromising delivery.
